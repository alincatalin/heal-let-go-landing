If you're anything like most people going through a breakup, you're probably looking for a neat little roadmap that tells you exactly how long this will take and when you'll feel normal again.

I'm not going to give you that. Because that's not how healing works.

But what I will give you is a framework for understanding what's actually happening in your mind.

## The 7 Stages (States) of a Breakup

1. **Shock:** Your brain's circuit breaker. You feel numb and detached.
2. **Denial:** Rehearsing conversations where they realize they made a mistake.
3. **Bargaining:** The "what if" loop. Convincing yourself that if you'd just done X, they'd still be here.
4. **Anger:** Creating separation. Anger gives you energy and detaches the bond.
5. **Depression:** The weight of reality. The internal mourning of the relationship and the future you planned.
6. **Testing:** Starting to experiment with life without them. New routines, new hobbies.
7. **Acceptance:** Peace. Not "over it," but no longer organized around the pain.

## Why You're Not Following the Map

You might feel three stages in one day. You might skip one and hit it six months later. This is normal. The stages aren't a checklist; they're a vocabulary.

If you're moving, even slowly, you're healing. Trust the process.
