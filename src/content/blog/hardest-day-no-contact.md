You made it through day one. Then day two. You thought you were doing okay. Then day three hit.

And suddenly, everything feels impossible. Your phone feels like it's burning a hole through your pocket.

## The 72-Hour Crash

By day three, the initial shock has worn off. The adrenaline that carried you through days one and two has faded. Your brain is in full withdrawal mode—literally using the same neural pathways as substance withdrawal.

Your cortisol (stress hormone) is high. Your dopamine receptors are screaming for the reward they’re used to.

## Survival Guide for Day 3

1. **Morning:** Don't reach for your phone first thing. Leave it across the room.
2. **Afternoon:** Interrupt the rumination loop. Change your physical environment. Go for a walk.
3. **Evening:** Structural barriers. Leave your phone in another room during dinner. Call a friend (not your ex).
4. **Night:** Nothing good comes from texting your ex at 2am. Airplane mode is your best friend.

## What to Remember

"This feeling is temporary. This decision is permanent." 

If you can get through today, tomorrow will be different. Not necessarily easier, but the pattern will start to change. You just have to get through this one.
