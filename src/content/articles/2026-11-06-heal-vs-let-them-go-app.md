## What Does Each App Do?

### Let Them Go

Let Them Go turns no contact into something you look after every day. Its listings describe:

- **A plant that grows with you.** "Water your pet plant daily and watch it grow with you as you maintain no contact."
- **A no contact streak** that tracks the last time you were in touch with your ex.
- **Healing tools,** including "Send to the Void," "Letter to Myself" and a "Red Flags List."
- **Quotes, affirmations, nature sounds and breathing tools.**
- **Premium extras:** panic button exercises for the urge to reach out, mood and journal logging, recovery lessons and progress charts.

### Heal

Heal is built around one moment: you're about to text your ex. Its main parts:

- **Unsent texts.** A chat that looks like theirs. You write the message, it never sends, and an AI coach replies instead and asks what you're hoping they'd say back.
- **SOS button.** One tap when the urge hits: breathe for a minute, then write it out, read your reasons or talk to the coach.
- **No contact tracker.** If you slip, your streak restarts, but your total and every day you made it stay on the calendar.
- **AI coach, private journal and a phase-by-phase path** that shows what's normal where you are.

The full feature list is on [What is Heal?](/blog/articles/what-is-heal/)

## Is Let Them Go Free?

Partly. The download is free, and its Google Play listing names the free part: the tracker and day counter, the plant, nature sounds and breathing, daily quotes and healing tools like red flags and "message into the void."

The tools for the hard moments are paid. The panic button, mood and journal logging and the charts are Premium, and the App Store listing says these need a Pro subscription. Some Google Play reviewers say they hit the paywall quickly, so expect to see a plan early.

Heal's free tier keeps the no contact tracker, the SOS button, the journal and 5 AI coach messages a day, for as long as you like. Premium adds 25x more coach messages, Streak Protection and every healing practice.

## Which One Costs Less?

**For a year, Let Them Go is cheaper.** Its App Store listing shows yearly plans from $4.99 to $39.99 (the price you're offered depends on the paywall), against $124.99 for Heal.

**For the urge to text, Heal's free tier goes further.** In Let Them Go, the panic button is Premium. In Heal, the SOS button and the journal are free, and so are 5 coach messages a day.

**Weekly plans add up.** Let Them Go's weekly plan is $4.99, about $260 over a year. If you'll keep it for months, pick a yearly plan in either app.

## How Do They Handle Your Data?

App Store privacy labels are filled in by each developer. Let Them Go's label says health and fitness data, contact info, user content and identifiers are linked to you, and that no data is used to track you. Heal's label lists its data as not linked to you, with no tracking.

What you write about your ex is about as personal as it gets, so it's worth a look. Both labels are on each app's App Store page.
