You go to send a message, or check their profile, and it's gone. No photo, no last seen, nothing. They blocked you.

It can feel like a slap: final, cold, and unfair, especially if you weren't even planning to reach out. Here's what blocking usually means, and what to do with the feelings it leaves.

## Why Did My Ex Block Me?

**Most people block an ex to protect themselves, not to punish you.** Blocking takes away the option to check, reply or give in, which can be the only way some people manage to move on.

The most common reasons:

| Reason | What it usually looks like | What it means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| They need distance to heal | Block soon after the breakup, often everywhere | They're struggling too, in their own way |
| They don't trust themselves | They know they'd check your profile or reply | You still affect them, but they've chosen space |
| They want contact to end | Block after repeated messages or arguments | A clear boundary to respect |
| They're angry or hurt | Block after a fight or something you said | It may soften with time, or it may not |
| A new partner asked | Block around the time they start dating | It's about their new relationship, not you |
| They're avoiding a hard talk | Block instead of explaining | It says more about how they handle conflict |

If you messaged them many times after the breakup, it's worth being honest with yourself: the block may be their way of saying they need it to stop. That's hard to hear, and it still needs respecting.

## Why Does Being Blocked Hurt So Much?

**Because it takes away your last bit of control.** Even if you weren't texting them, knowing you could was a small comfort. Blocking removes that, and it can feel like being rejected a second time.

It also cuts off the checking. Every look at their profile gave your brain a small hit of connection, and now that's gone too. The first days can feel like withdrawal. Brain scans of people still in love after a breakup show activity in reward and craving areas linked to addiction ([Fisher et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00784.2009)). That's why the urge to find a way around the block can feel so strong.

The upside is real, though. Checking their profile keeps you stuck. Being blocked does some of the hardest work of no contact for you.

## What Should I Do When My Ex Blocks Me?

1. **Don't try to get around it.** No new accounts, no messages through friends, no calls from another number, no showing up. It will almost always confirm why they blocked you, and it can cross into harassment.
2. **Don't post at them.** Cryptic quotes and sad songs aimed at your ex are messages in disguise, and mutual friends notice.
3. **Say what you need to say somewhere safe.** You still have words that need to go somewhere. Write them in our free [unsent text tool](/tools/unsent-text/) or an [unsent letter](/blog/articles/unsent-letter-to-ex/).
4. **Decide what you'll do about them.** Do you want to mute or block them back on the apps where you still see them? Our guide on [whether to block your ex](/blog/articles/should-i-block-my-ex/) can help you decide calmly.
5. **Count it as day one.** You're in no contact now, whether you chose it or not. Our [no contact rule guide](/blog/articles/no-contact-rule-guide/) explains what the next weeks tend to look like, and the [no contact counter](/tools/no-contact-counter/) keeps track.
6. **Tell a friend.** Being blocked can feel humiliating, and shame grows in silence. Saying "they blocked me and it hurts" out loud helps.

## Does Blocking Mean They Never Cared?

**No.** People often block someone precisely because they cared and can't handle seeing them. Blocking can be a sign of strong feelings as much as weak ones.

But it also doesn't mean they secretly want you back. The honest answer is that a block tells you very little about their feelings, and a lot about what they need right now: distance. Trying to decode it further usually keeps you stuck.

## Will My Ex Unblock Me?

Sometimes. People do unblock exes, often weeks or months later, when the feelings have calmed. Others never do.

If they unblock you, it doesn't automatically mean they want to talk. It might be a moment of curiosity, or a sign they feel safe enough not to need the block anymore. Read [why your ex unblocked you](/blog/articles/ex-unblocked-me/) before you decide what to do.

Either way, don't build your days around waiting for it. Checking whether you're still blocked is just checking their profile in another form.

## What If We Need to Talk About Practical Things?

If you share a lease, a pet, belongings or bills, you may still need one way to communicate.

- Use a channel that's still open, like email, and keep it short and factual.
- If every channel is blocked, ask one trusted mutual friend to pass on a single practical message, once.
- If it's about money or property you can't sort out, get advice from a legal or tenancy service instead of chasing them.

For moving their things back and forth, see [what to do with your ex's stuff](/blog/articles/what-to-do-with-your-exs-stuff/).

## FAQ: When Your Ex Blocks You

**Should I reach out after my ex blocked me?**
No. A block is a clear sign they don't want contact right now. Reaching out another way usually makes things worse and can feel like harassment to them. Write it down for yourself instead.

**Why did my ex block me if they dumped me?**
Often because ending it was hard for them too, and seeing your posts or messages makes it harder. Some people also block to stop themselves from changing their mind. It's about their healing, not a statement that you didn't matter.

**Why did my ex block me and then unblock me?**
Usually because their feelings shifted: the anger cooled, or they felt safe enough to drop the wall. It doesn't necessarily mean they want contact. See [why your ex unblocked you](/blog/articles/ex-unblocked-me/).

**Is my ex blocking me a sign they still love me?**
It can be, but it's not reliable either way. Some people block because their feelings are strong, others because they want a clean break. Don't build hope on it.

**Should I block my ex back?**
Only if it helps you. If you keep checking whether you're still blocked, or seeing them on other apps hurts, blocking or muting back can bring peace. Do it for you, not to make a point.

**How do I stop obsessing after my ex blocked me?**
Give the urge somewhere else to go: an unsent message, a walk, a friend to text. Count the days since the block. The obsession eases as the craving does. Our guide on [how to stop thinking about your ex](/blog/articles/how-to-stop-thinking-about-your-ex/) has more.
