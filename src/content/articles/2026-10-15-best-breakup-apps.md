## How We Compared Them

We looked at each app's App Store listing, pricing and what reviewers say about it, and asked five questions:

1. **Does it help in the moment?** The hardest part of a breakup is the minute before you text, call or check their profile.
2. **Does it track no contact?** Seeing the days add up is motivating, and most apps in this space do it.
3. **Is there somewhere private to write?** Writing it out is one of the most useful things you can do after a breakup ([Pennebaker, 1997](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1997.tb00403.x)).
4. **What does it cost, and when do you hit the paywall?**
5. **Does it point you to real help when you need it?**

## 2. Get Over Him: Breakup Healing

**Best for:** a low price over a year.

Get Over Him is one of the most-reviewed apps in the category and is aimed squarely at women getting over a man. It focuses on no contact and healing from heartbreak.

**Price:** $4.99 a week, $14.99 a month or $18.99 a year. The annual plan is the cheapest of the paid options here.

**Worth knowing:** the most common complaint in its reviews is a hard paywall straight after onboarding, so expect to choose a plan early. If you know you'll use an app for months, the annual price is hard to beat.

## 3. Breakup Buddy

**Best for:** talking things through with an AI.

Breakup Buddy has been around since 2023, longer than most apps here, and its focus is an AI you can chat with about your ex and the breakup.

**Price:** plans listed at $17.99 and $42.99.

**Worth knowing:** if what you want most is a conversation, any time of day, this is a focused option. It leans less on in-the-moment tools for the urge to text.

## 4. No Contact Tracker: BreakUp

**Best for:** paying once and being done with it.

As the name says, this app is built around tracking your no contact streak. It's available in six languages and was updated recently.

**Price:** $7.99 a week, or $29.99 for lifetime access.

**Worth knowing:** the lifetime option is good value if you want a tracker for a long stretch. Avoid the weekly plan unless you only need it briefly; it adds up fast.

## 5. Breakup App: Let it Go

**Best for:** wanting other people, not just an app.

Let it Go combines a no contact tracker with a community of people going through the same thing, plus paid chats with human "healers." It's updated very often.

**Price:** $9.99 a month or $69 a year, with human support sessions on top.

**Worth knowing:** if feeling less alone is what you need most, the community side sets it apart. Human support isn't the same as therapy, so check who you're talking to.

## 6. No Contact App

## Do Breakup Apps Actually Work?

An app can't make you get over someone. Time, people who care about you and, for some, a therapist do most of that work.

What an app can do is change what happens in the hard moments. Checking an ex's profile, for example, is linked to more distress and longing and less personal growth after a breakup ([Marshall, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2012.0125)). A tool that gives that urge somewhere else to go helps you stop feeding the loop.

So the best breakup app is the one you'll actually open at 2am instead of their chat. If that's a notes app and a friend who picks up, that works too. Our list of [what to do instead of texting your ex](/blog/articles/what-to-do-instead-of-texting-your-ex/) has twelve free ideas.

## Are Breakup Apps Private?

Most of what you write in a breakup app is about as personal as it gets, so check before you start:

- Does the app say where your journal and chats are stored?
- Can you delete your data?
- Does it ask for contacts or social logins it doesn't need?

At Heal, your journal and unsent texts are private to you, and unsent texts never go to your ex. Read any app's privacy policy before you pour your heart into it.

## FAQ: Breakup Apps

**What is the best app to get over a breakup?**
It depends on what you struggle with most. For the urge to text your ex, Heal is built for that moment. For the lowest annual price, Get Over Him. For a free option, No Contact App. Try the free tier or trial first, since the right app is the one you'll actually open.

**Is there a free app to get over him?**
Yes. No Contact App is free, and Heal is free to download with a 7-day trial and a free tier after it. Most others have a short trial or a paywall soon after you sign up.

**Is there an app to stop texting my ex?**
Yes. Heal gives you a chat that looks like your ex's but never sends, plus an SOS button for when the urge hits. Writing the message out without sending it often takes the pressure off.

**What is the best no contact tracker app?**
Most breakup apps include one. For tracking alone, No Contact Tracker has a one-time lifetime price and No Contact App is free. Heal's tracker shows slips in context rather than resetting you to zero.

**Can a breakup app replace therapy?**
No. Breakup apps help with daily habits and hard moments, but they aren't therapy. If you're struggling to function or feel unsafe, talk to a doctor or therapist, or find a local helpline at findahelpline.com.
