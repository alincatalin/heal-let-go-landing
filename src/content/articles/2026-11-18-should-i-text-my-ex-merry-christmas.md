It's one line. "Merry Christmas." Polite, harmless, the kind of thing you'd send a coworker.

Except it isn't going to a coworker, and you've probably rewritten it six times. Here's how to decide.

## Is It Okay to Text My Ex Merry Christmas?

It depends on where you are. Here's the honest version.

| Where you are | Send it? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In no contact, any day count | No | It restarts contact, and you'll spend Christmas checking for a reply |
| Broke up in the last few months | Probably not | It's likely to open the conversation you're trying to close |
| Still hoping to get back together | No | It's a test of whether they miss you, and you'll read everything into the reply |
| They ended things | No | The next move, if there is one, is theirs |
| You share kids | Yes, short and about the kids | "Merry Christmas, hope the kids loved their presents" |
| Years later, genuinely friendly | Fine | A short message with no follow-up question |
| They were unkind or unsafe | No | You don't owe them a holiday greeting |

The same goes for Happy New Year, with one extra risk: it's usually sent after midnight, often after a few drinks.

## What Is the Text Really For?

Be honest with yourself before you send it. A holiday text to an ex is rarely just a greeting.

Usually it's one of these:

- **A check.** You want to see if they reply, how fast, and how warmly.
- **An opening.** You're hoping it turns into a conversation.
- **A wound.** You want them to feel the gap you feel.
- **A goodbye.** You want closure and this feels like a gentle way to get it.

If the part you care about is what they send back, it isn't really a Merry Christmas. It's a question, and the reply is the answer you're waiting on.

That matters because contact keeps the feelings going. In a study that followed young adults through the weeks after a breakup, those who stayed in contact with their ex saw their love and sadness fade more slowly ([Sbarra and Emery, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1350-4126.2005.00112.x)). One "Merry Christmas" is the easiest way back into contact there is.

## Why Is the Urge So Strong at Christmas?

Because the season is built from the cues you shared: the songs, the lights, the traditions, the family you used to visit together.

Missing an ex isn't only sadness. In a brain-imaging study of people who'd recently been rejected, seeing a photo of the ex activated reward and craving systems in the brain, the same kinds of systems involved in addiction ([Fisher et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00784.2009)). Christmas is a month of reminders. The urge isn't a sign you should text. It's a sign the reminders are working.

1. **Decide now.** "I'm not texting them over the holidays" is easier to keep if you decided in early December.

## What If My Ex Texts Me Merry Christmas First?

You don't have to reply on the day, or at all.

First, it might be hoovering, breadcrumbing, or a sincere hello. A two-minute check with the [is my ex hoovering me quiz](/tools/is-my-ex-hoovering-me/) helps you tell which.

Then pick the reply that protects your peace:

| Situation | Reply |
| --- | --- |
| In no contact | No reply. Or, if silence feels wrong, a short one a few days later: "Thanks, you too." |
| Co-parenting | "Merry Christmas. The kids had a great day." Keep it about them. |
| They want something more | "Thanks. I'm still not in a place to talk." |
| Friendly and long past it | "Merry Christmas, hope it's a good one." Then put the phone down. |

A reply with a question in it ("How's your family?") is an invitation to keep talking. Leave the question off unless you want the conversation.

## Should I Text My Ex Happy New Year?

The same answer as Christmas, with two extra things to watch.

- **The time.** "Happy New Year" texts often go out after midnight, after a few drinks. Alcohol narrows your attention to what's in front of you and pushes out the bigger picture ([Steele and Josephs, 1990](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.45.8.921)). What's in front of you is your phone.
- **The meaning.** "New year" feels like a fresh start, so a Happy New Year text can sound like "can we start over?" even if you don't mean it that way.

## FAQ: Texting Your Ex Merry Christmas or Happy New Year

**Should I text my ex Merry Christmas if we're in no contact?**
No. A holiday text breaks no contact and tends to restart a conversation. Research links ongoing contact after a breakup with slower recovery. Write it somewhere it can't send and decide after Christmas.

**Is it rude not to text my ex Merry Christmas?**
No. After a breakup, not texting is normal and usually kind to both of you. Silence isn't an insult. It's space.

**What should I say if my ex texts me Merry Christmas?**
You don't have to reply. If you want to, keep it short and with no question, like "Thanks, you too," and send it later, not on the day.

**Should I text my ex Happy New Year at midnight?**
Usually not. Midnight texts are often sent after drinks and can sound like an invitation to start over. Wait until the morning, and if you still want to, take a minute to ask what you're hoping for.

**Can I text my ex's family Merry Christmas?**
A short reply if they reach out is fine. If you're tempted to message them first, ask whether part of you hopes it gets back to your ex. If it does, wait.

**What if I already sent the Merry Christmas text?**
That's okay. It doesn't erase the days you made it. Don't follow up, don't keep checking for a reply, and start counting again from tomorrow.
