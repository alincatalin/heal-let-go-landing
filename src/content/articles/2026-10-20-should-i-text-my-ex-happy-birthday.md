You've known the date for weeks. Maybe it's in your calendar, maybe it's just in your head. And now it's here, and a two-word text feels like the most normal thing in the world.

It might be. Or it might be the thing that undoes a month of hard work. It depends on where you are.

## Is It Okay to Text My Ex Happy Birthday?

Here's the honest version, by situation.

| Where you are | Send it? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In no contact | No | It breaks the rule and usually starts a conversation |
| Still hoping to get back together | Better not | You'll be waiting for the reply all day |
| Broke up recently (under a few months) | Wait | It's too soon to know what it means to you |
| Long past it, friendly now | Yes, keep it short | "Happy birthday, hope it's a good one" |
| Co-parenting or shared pets | Yes, if it's your norm | Keep it warm and brief, no more |

A simple test: if they didn't reply at all, would your day be ruined? If yes, the text isn't really a birthday wish. It's a question.

## Why Does Their Birthday Hit So Hard?

Because it's a day you used to plan for. The gift, the dinner, the little rituals that were only yours. Now the date arrives with nowhere to put any of that.

It's also one of the few days when reaching out feels allowed. It's polite, and it's only one line. That's exactly why it's tempting, and why it's worth slowing down.

## What If They Texted Me on My Birthday?

Then it's a little harder, because now it feels rude not to return it.

You don't owe a matching text. If they wished you well and you're in no contact, silence is still fine. If you want to acknowledge it, wait a day or two and keep it to one line: "Thanks for the birthday message." Then stop. ## What Do I Do With the Urge on the Day?

Plan the day before it arrives. A birthday you didn't plan for is the one you spend staring at your phone.

- **Decide the night before.** "I'm not texting tomorrow" is easier to keep if it isn't a decision you make at 9pm.
- **Mute their profile for the day.** Their friends will post. You don't need to see it.
- **Fill the evening.** Dinner with a friend, a film, anything with other people in it.

## FAQ: Texting Your Ex on Their Birthday

**Should I text my ex happy birthday during no contact?**
No. A birthday text breaks no contact and usually opens a conversation, and you'll likely spend the day waiting for the reply. Write it somewhere it can't send instead.

**Is it rude not to wish my ex happy birthday?**
No. After a breakup, not reaching out is a normal boundary, not a snub. Most people understand silence from an ex.

**What should I text my ex on his birthday if we're still friends?**
Keep it short and warm: "Happy birthday, hope it's a good one." Skip memories, inside jokes or "I miss you," which turn a wish into an invitation.

**Should I text my ex happy birthday if they texted me on mine?**
You don't have to. If you want to acknowledge it, a one-line thank-you a day or two later is enough, without starting a back-and-forth.
