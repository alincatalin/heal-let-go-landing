They watched your story within a minute. They liked a photo from three years ago. A friend said they asked how you're doing.

It's hard not to read into it. You want to know: do they miss me? Do they want me back?

This guide goes through the common signs, what each one usually means, and why, even when the answer is "yes, they miss you", it might not change what you should do.

## How Can You Tell If Your Ex Misses You?

**You can see signs that they're thinking about you. You can't see whether they want the relationship back.** Story views, a text on your birthday or questions to mutual friends all mean you're on their mind. They don't tell you whether they've changed, or whether anything that ended it is different.

| The sign | What it might mean | What it doesn't mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watches your stories, often first | Curiosity, habit, checking how you're doing | That they want you back |
| Likes old photos or posts | Nostalgia, or a nudge to see if you respond | A real plan to reconnect |
| Texts late at night | Loneliness, a drink, a bad evening | Something they'll mean in the morning |
| Texts on birthdays and holidays | A low-risk way to stay in your life | A change of heart |
| Asks mutual friends about you | Wants to know if you're okay, or seeing someone | That they'll ask you directly |
| Keeps your things or their gifts | Sentiment, or just not dealing with it | That they're waiting for you |
| Shows up where you'll be | Wants to see you, or see your reaction | A sincere conversation |
| Says "I miss you" | They feel something right now | That anything has changed |
| Apologizes and names what went wrong | Real reflection | A promise. Watch what happens next |

The last row is different from the rest. It's the only one that's about the relationship, not about their feelings.

## Common Signs Your Ex Misses You

**They keep watching from a distance.** Story views, profile visits you can see, likes without messages. This pattern is often called [orbiting](/blog/articles/what-is-orbiting/). It keeps you aware of them without them having to say anything.

**They reach out with low-effort messages.** "hey", a meme, "saw this and thought of you". These keep a door open without committing to anything. If it's a pattern, read [what is breadcrumbing](/blog/articles/what-is-breadcrumbing/).

**They text on special dates.** Birthdays, Christmas, the anniversary. Those dates remind them of you, too. Our guides on [whether to reply to a birthday text](/blog/articles/should-i-text-my-ex-happy-birthday/) and [a Christmas text](/blog/articles/should-i-text-my-ex-merry-christmas/) cover what to do.

**They ask about you.** Through friends, family, or a casual "how's work?". They want to know how you are, and often whether you've moved on.

**They bring up memories.** "Remember when we...". Nostalgia is real, but it's about the past, not the future.

**They're friendly but vague about why.** They want to "stay friends", but the messages feel like more. In research on why people stay friends with an ex, sentimental reasons ranked highest, but continued romantic attraction was also one of the reasons people gave ([Mogilski and Welling, 2017](https://www.bps.org.uk/research-digest/reasons-we-stay-friends-ex)).

**They're jealous or ask if you're seeing someone.** That's about them not wanting to lose you, which isn't the same as wanting you.

## Signs Your Ex Wants You Back (Not Just Misses You)

Missing someone is a feeling. Wanting to get back together is a plan. These are the signs that point to a plan:

- **They ask to talk properly**, in person, with time, not at 1am.
- **They name what went wrong**, including their part, without blaming only you.
- **They've changed something concrete**, and you can see it over weeks, not just hear about it.
- **They respect your answer**, even if it's "not now" or "no".
- **They're consistent.** No hot-and-cold, no disappearing for a week.

## Signs an Avoidant Ex Misses You

People with an avoidant style often pull away when things get close, then reach out once there's distance. The signs tend to be quieter:

- Watching but not messaging
- Practical excuses to get in touch ("did I leave my charger?")
- Warm messages followed by silence
- Reaching out when they feel safe that you won't ask for much

An avoidant ex missing you is real, but the push-pull usually comes back too. If you'd be getting back into the same cycle, ask yourself whether the closeness you wanted would ever arrive.

## Why the Signs Matter Less Than You Think

Here's the hard part. Even if your ex does miss you, that doesn't tell you what to do.

- **Missing you doesn't fix the reason it ended.** People mostly reunite because of lingering feelings ([Dailey et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1080/00224545.2010.503249)), and on-again, off-again couples tend to report less satisfaction and more uncertainty ([Dailey, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108567756.003)).
- **Decoding signs keeps you in the loop.** Every check of their story view list is a small hit of hope, then a crash. People who kept checking an ex's profile reported more distress and longing ([Marshall, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2012.0125)).
- **The signs pull you away from your own healing.** Hours spent decoding are hours not spent on your life.

The better question isn't "do they miss me?" It's "what do I want, whether or not they do?" If you're seriously weighing a second try, use the [10 questions in should I get back with my ex?](/blog/articles/should-i-get-back-with-my-ex/)

1. **Pause.** Don't react in the moment, especially not at night.

## FAQ: Signs Your Ex Misses You

**Does my ex miss me if they watch my stories?**
They're curious about you, and you're on their mind. It doesn't mean they want to talk or get back together. For many people, watching stories is a habit that takes no effort.

**Does no contact make your ex miss you?**
Sometimes, but that shouldn't be the reason you do it. No contact is for your healing. If it makes them miss you, that's a side effect, not a goal. More in [will my ex come back?](/blog/articles/will-my-ex-come-back/)

**What are the signs your ex is not over you?**
Frequent contact, asking about your dating life, jealousy, and keeping your things. These show unresolved feelings. Whether they'll act on them, and whether that would be good for you, are separate questions.

**Why does my ex keep texting me if they don't want me back?**
Because staying in touch is easier than letting go, and texting you may comfort them. It can also be a way to keep you available. If it hurts, it's okay to stop replying or to [block your ex](/blog/articles/should-i-block-my-ex/).

**How do I stop looking for signs my ex misses me?**
Mute them, hide your stories from them, ask friends not to pass on news, and write down what you want to ask them instead of sending it. The urge to check fades the less you feed it.

**Do exes who miss you come back?**
Some do. Those who come back and stay usually show real, lasting change, not just missing you. See [will my ex come back?](/blog/articles/will-my-ex-come-back/) for when and why exes return.
