Ninety days. Three months ago, getting through an evening without reaching out felt impossible. Now there are probably whole days when you don't think about them at all.

And then a song comes on, or you hear their name, and it's all there again for an afternoon. Here's what three months of no contact usually changes, what it doesn't, and how to decide what comes next.

## What Happens After 3 Months of No Contact?

**For most people, three months is when the breakup stops running the day.** The craving has calmed, the thoughts are less frequent, and you can look at the relationship without only seeing the best parts.

That fits what research shows about how breakup feelings fade with time and distance. In a study that tracked young adults daily after a breakup, love and sadness for the ex both eased over the following weeks, and they eased more slowly in people who stayed in contact ([Sbarra and Emery, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1350-4126.2005.00112.x)). Ninety days of no contact gives that process real room.

| | Day 30 | Day 90 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thinking about them | Most days, often | Some days, briefly |
| The urge to text | Still strong at night or after triggers | Rare, usually tied to a specific trigger |
| How you see the relationship | Starting to see the bad parts | More balanced, both good and bad |
| Your routines | Rebuilding | Mostly your own again |
| Bad days | Can flatten you | Pass faster, and you know they pass |

If you're earlier in the process, see [day 30 of no contact](/blog/articles/no-contact-day-30/) or the full [no contact day-by-day guide](/blog/articles/no-contact-day-by-day/).

## Why Do I Still Miss My Ex After 3 Months?

**Because three months is a milestone, not a finish line.** Missing someone after a meaningful relationship can last longer, and that's normal.

Some common reasons it comes back:

- **Dates and seasons.** Anniversaries, birthdays and holidays bring the memories back. If one is coming up, plan the day with people.
- **News about them.** Hearing they're dating someone, or seeing a photo, can reopen it for a day or two.
- **A hard patch in your own life.** When work or family is tough, your mind reaches for the person who used to comfort you.
- **The relationship was long.** Long relationships take longer to heal. See [how to get over a long-term relationship](/blog/articles/how-to-get-over-a-long-term-relationship/).

A wave at three months isn't a sign you've gone backwards. Notice how fast it passes compared with month one. For more on the missing, read [why do I miss my ex](/blog/articles/why-do-i-miss-my-ex/) and [how to stop missing someone](/blog/articles/how-to-stop-missing-someone/).

If you still can't sleep, eat or function normally after three months, that's worth talking to a doctor or therapist about. One study found that sleep problems lasting beyond about ten weeks after a separation were linked to rising blood pressure ([Krietsch et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1037/hea0000089)). Our guide to [depression after a breakup](/blog/articles/depression-after-a-breakup/) explains the signs.

## What Does Their Silence Mean After 3 Months?

**Usually, that they're moving on too, or that they're respecting the distance.** Three months without a word from either side tells you the relationship has ended in practice, not just in words.

It doesn't mean you didn't matter. Some people never reach out because it would hurt too much, or because they think it's kinder not to. Others have moved on. You can't know which from the outside, and our post on [what he's thinking during no contact](/blog/articles/what-is-he-thinking-during-no-contact/) explains why trying to guess keeps you stuck.

If you're still hoping they'll come back, read [will my ex come back](/blog/articles/will-my-ex-come-back/) for an honest look at the odds.

## Should I Reach Out After 3 Months of No Contact?

**Only if you'd be fine with any answer, including none.** At 90 days you're calmer than you were, which makes this a better time to decide than day 3. But it's still a decision, not something the calendar makes for you.

- [ ] Could I handle a short reply, or silence?
- [ ] Am I thinking about this because of a trigger, like a date or news about them?
- [ ] Have I felt this way for weeks, or only today?

## What If They Reach Out After 3 Months?

Some exes do get in touch around this time, often casually: a "hey," a meme, a "thinking of you." It can feel huge after so long.

Take your time. Wait at least a day before replying. Look at what they actually said, and decide what you want first. Our guide on [what to do when your ex texts you](/blog/articles/my-ex-texted-me/) has replies for each case. If the message is warm but vague and comes when you're doing well, it may be [hoovering](/blog/articles/what-is-hoovering/).

## What Should I Do After 90 Days?

You have three good options:

1. **Keep going.** No contact doesn't have to end at 90 days. Many people simply keep the distance as their normal. Use our [how long should no contact last calculator](/tools/how-long-no-contact/) if you want a target.
2. **Move to low contact.** If you share friends, work or kids, you might settle into polite, brief contact on practical things only.
3. **Close the door.** Unfollow, delete what's left, and stop counting. Not out of anger, but because you don't need the count anymore.

Whatever you choose, mark the milestone. A meal, a trip, something new. Ninety days of choosing yourself is worth celebrating.

## FAQ: 3 Months of No Contact

**Is 3 months of no contact enough?**
For many people, yes. Three months is often enough to see the relationship clearly and stop wanting contact most of the time. If you still feel a strong pull, keeping going is fine. There's no maximum.

**Will my ex come back after 3 months of no contact?**
Some do, many don't. By three months, silence usually means they're moving on. No contact isn't a strategy to get them back, so don't measure your progress by whether they reach out.

**Is it normal to still think about my ex after 90 days?**
Yes. What usually changes is how often and how strongly. Brief thoughts that pass quickly are a sign of healing, not a sign you're stuck.

**Does 90 days of no contact work?**
It works on you. Ninety days gives the craving time to calm and gives you a clearer view of what you want. Whether your ex reaches out is a different question, and not a good measure of success.

**Should I break no contact on their birthday after 3 months?**
Usually no. A birthday message often reopens the door and leaves you waiting for a reply. Read [should I text my ex happy birthday](/blog/articles/should-i-text-my-ex-happy-birthday/) before you decide.

**What if I broke no contact before 3 months?**
Then start counting again from today. Your days before the slip still count toward healing. See [what to do after you break no contact](/blog/articles/i-broke-no-contact/).
