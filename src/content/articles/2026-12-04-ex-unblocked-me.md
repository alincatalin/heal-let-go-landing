You notice their profile picture is back. Or your message would go through now. Or they've watched your story. After weeks of silence, your ex unblocked you, and your heart is racing.

Before you read anything into it, or type anything, here's what an unblock usually means and how to handle it without losing the progress you've made.

## Why Did My Ex Unblock Me?

**Most of the time, an unblock means their feelings have cooled enough that they don't need the wall anymore.** It's a change in how they feel about the block, not necessarily about you.

| Possible reason | What's likely going on | What it means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| They've calmed down | The anger or hurt that caused the block has faded | They feel less reactive, not necessarily closer |
| They feel safe | They no longer worry they'd give in and contact you | Often a sign they're moving on |
| They're curious | They want to see how you're doing | Curiosity isn't the same as wanting you back |
| They want to look mature | Blocking started to feel petty | A tidy-up, not a message |
| They're testing the waters | They want to see if you'll reach out | A low-effort way to get attention without asking |
| They're thinking of reaching out | They want the option open | Wait and see what they actually do |
| It's practical | Something shared needs sorting | Expect a short, factual message, if any |

Notice that only a few of those involve wanting you back. An unblock is a small, private action, and most of the reasons are about their own comfort.

## What Does It Mean If They Unblocked Me but Didn't Text?

**It usually means they're not ready, or not planning, to start a conversation.** If they wanted to talk, the unblock would likely come with a message.

Some exes unblock and then watch your stories, like an old post or follow you again without saying anything. That pattern has names. Watching from a distance is often called [orbiting](/blog/articles/what-is-orbiting/). Small bits of attention with no real effort are [breadcrumbing](/blog/articles/what-is-breadcrumbing/). Coming back with warmth after a long silence, especially when you've moved on, can be [hoovering](/blog/articles/what-is-hoovering/).

None of these tell you they want a relationship. They tell you they want a connection that costs them very little. If you're not sure which one you're seeing, our free [is my ex hoovering me quiz](/tools/is-my-ex-hoovering-me/) helps you look at it more calmly.

## Should I Message My Ex After They Unblocked Me?

**Usually not.** Messaging first undoes the no contact you've built, and it puts you back in the position of waiting for their reply.

- [ ] Am I only thinking about this because of the unblock?
- [ ] Is it late at night, or have I been drinking?

## What If They Message Me After Unblocking?

Then you get to decide, and you don't have to decide fast.

- **Wait before you reply.** A day is fine. There's no prize for answering quickly.
- **Look at what they actually said.** "Hey" or "I saw this and thought of you" is low effort. An apology with specifics, or a clear request to talk about what went wrong, is different.
- **Decide what you want, not what they want.** Do you want to get back together, be friendly, or close the door? Our guide on [what to do when your ex texts you](/blog/articles/my-ex-texted-me/) has replies for each.
- **Keep your no contact count honest.** If they message and you don't reply, your streak hasn't broken. If you reply, that's okay too, just be clear about why.

## How Do I Stop Checking Whether I'm Blocked?

Checking whether you're blocked is checking their profile with extra steps. Each look gives you a jolt, and each jolt resets the craving.

- **Mute or block them yourself.** If you can see them, you'll keep looking. Our guide on [whether to block your ex](/blog/articles/should-i-block-my-ex/) can help you decide.
- **Move their chat out of sight.** Archive it so the unblock isn't the first thing you see.
- **Count days without checking,** not only days without texting. Our [no contact counter](/tools/no-contact-counter/) works for both.
- **Plan for the trigger.** The urge to check usually hits at night, after a bad day, or when you hear news about them. Have a friend to text instead.

If they blocked you in the first place and you're still processing it, our post on [what to do when your ex blocks you](/blog/articles/my-ex-blocked-me/) covers that part.

## Does an Unblock Mean No Contact Worked?

**Not in the way people hope.** No contact isn't a trick to make them come back. It's time for you to heal and see the relationship clearly.

If they unblock you, that's their choice. Your progress is the days you didn't reach out, the nights you got through, and how much less their name affects you. That's still true whatever they do next. ## FAQ: When Your Ex Unblocks You

**Why would my ex unblock me after months?**
Usually because enough time has passed that the block no longer feels necessary. They've calmed down, or moved on enough not to worry about seeing you. Sometimes it's curiosity. It doesn't usually mean they're about to reach out.

**Does unblocking mean they want me back?**
Not on its own. Someone who wants you back usually says so clearly. An unblock without a message is more often a sign that their strong feelings have faded.

**He unblocked me during no contact. Should I break it?**
No. His unblock doesn't change why you started no contact. If he wants to talk, he can message. Keep your streak and see what he does, not what you hope he means.

**Why did my ex unblock me and then block me again?**
Usually mixed feelings: they missed seeing you, or felt curious, then found it hurt or tempted them. It shows they're still unsettled. It's not a reason for you to reach out.

**Should I unfollow or block them now that they've unblocked me?**
If seeing them keeps pulling you back in, yes. Muting or blocking on your side protects your progress. You don't owe them access just because they gave it back.

**My ex unblocked me and watched my story. What does it mean?**
They're curious about your life. That's all it reliably means. Don't change what you post to get their attention. Post for yourself, or not at all.
