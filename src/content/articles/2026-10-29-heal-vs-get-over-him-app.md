## What Does Each App Do?

### Get Over Him

Get Over Him is built for women getting over a man. Its listing describes four parts:

- **Text him without texting him.** A text simulator for the "I miss you" text, which, in its words, "shows you exactly how it would go."
- **Mochi.** An AI "best friend" to vent to at 2am.
- **No contact streak.** Track your streak and watch your progress.
- **Daily reminders.** A home screen widget that nudges you to stay focused on you.

### Heal

Heal is built around one moment: you're about to text your ex. Its main parts:

- **Unsent texts.** A chat that looks like theirs. You write the message, it never sends, and an AI coach replies instead and asks what you're hoping they'd say back.
- **SOS button.** One tap when the urge hits: breathe for a minute, then write it out, read your reasons or talk to the coach.
- **No contact tracker.** If you slip, your streak restarts, but your total and every day you made it stay on the calendar.
- **AI coach, private journal and a phase-by-phase path** that shows what's normal where you are.

The full feature list is on [What is Heal?](/blog/articles/what-is-heal/)

## Which One Costs Less?

Get Over Him's listing shows two different monthly prices ($14.99 and $29.99), so check which one the paywall offers you.

## How Do They Handle Your Data?

App Store privacy labels are filled in by each developer. Get Over Him's label says contact info, user content, identifiers and usage data are linked to you, and that usage data is used to track you. Heal's label lists the same kinds of data as not linked to you, with no tracking.

If what you write to your ex in an app is something you'd want kept away from your identity, that difference is worth a look. Both labels are on each app's App Store page.

## FAQ: Get Over Him App vs Heal

**Is the Get Over Him app free?**
No. Its App Store listing says "You need to pay to use the app. There are no free features." Plans start at $4.99 a week.

**Is there a free app like Get Over Him?**
Heal has a free tier that keeps the no contact tracker, SOS button, journal and 5 AI coach messages a day. You can also try the free [unsent text tool](/tools/unsent-text/) in your browser, with no app at all.

**Is Get Over Him on Android?**
Its App Store listing covers iPhone, Mac and Vision Pro, and we couldn't confirm an Android version. Heal is on both the App Store and Google Play.

**Do both apps let me text my ex without sending it?**
Yes. Get Over Him has a text simulator, and Heal has unsent texts, where an AI coach replies instead of your ex. In both, nothing reaches him.

**Which app is better for no contact?**
Both track your no contact streak. Heal also keeps your total days on a calendar after a slip and adds an SOS button for the urge. If you're new to it, start with our [no contact rule guide](/blog/articles/no-contact-rule-guide/).
