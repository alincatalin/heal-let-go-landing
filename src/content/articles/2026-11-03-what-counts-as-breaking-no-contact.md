No contact sounds simple until you're holding your phone, wondering if a heart on their post counts. Here's a clear line, and an honest look at the grey areas.

The rule of thumb: **if they could notice it came from you, it's contact.**

## What Counts as Breaking No Contact?

Anything that reaches them, or invites them to reach you:

- Texts, calls, DMs, emails and voice notes, including "accidental" ones
- Replying to their message, even with one word or an emoji
- Liking, commenting on or reacting to their posts and stories
- Sending something through a friend ("tell them I said hi")
- Showing up where you know they'll be, to be seen
- Posting something aimed at them, like a song lyric they'll recognise
- Drunk calls, late night "u up?" texts and happy birthday messages

If you've done one of these, it's not the end of your progress. Read [what to do after you break no contact](/blog/articles/i-broke-no-contact/).

## What Doesn't Count?

Some contact is necessary. It doesn't break no contact if you keep it short, practical and about the task:

- Co-parenting arrangements and messages about the kids
- Returning belongings, sorting out a lease, bills or a shared pet
- Work conversations you can't avoid (see [no contact with a coworker](/blog/articles/no-contact-with-coworker/))
- An emergency

Keep these to the facts. "I'll drop the box off Saturday at 10" is logistics. "I'll drop the box off Saturday. How have you been?" opens the door.

## The Grey Areas

| Situation | Is it contact? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liking their post | Yes | They get a notification with your name on it |
| Watching their story | Yes, treat it as contact | Your name shows up in their viewer list, and it keeps you hooked |
| Checking their profile without them knowing | No | But it restarts the hurt each time. See [stop checking their Instagram](/blog/articles/stop-checking-ex-instagram/) |
| Reading old messages | No | It's not contact, but it can keep you stuck |
| They text you and you don't reply | No | Not replying keeps your no contact intact |
| They text you and you reply | Yes | A reply is contact, even a short one |
| Wishing them happy birthday | Yes | See [should I text my ex happy birthday](/blog/articles/should-i-text-my-ex-happy-birthday/) |
| Talking about them with mutual friends | No | Unless you're asking friends to pass a message on |
| Unblocking them "just to look" | No, but close | It's often the step right before a message |

## Why Do the Grey Areas Matter?

No contact isn't a rule for its own sake. It's there to give your mind a break from them so the pull can fade. That's why the "technically not contact" things still matter.

Checking who they follow, scrolling their old posts or rereading old chats doesn't reach them, but it does keep them at the center of your day. Each look restarts the same hope and the same hurt.

A good question to ask: **"Am I doing this to feel better, or to get a reaction or an update?"** If it's the second, treat it like contact.

## What If They Contact You?

Their message doesn't break your no contact. Your reply does. You can choose not to answer, or answer only the practical part.

## FAQ: Breaking No Contact

**Does viewing their story break no contact?**
Treat it as if it does. You're not sending anything, but your name shows up in their viewer list, and it keeps you checking. Muting their stories makes it easier.

**Does liking a post break no contact?**
Yes. A like sends them a notification with your name, which is a small way of saying "I'm still here."

**If my ex texts me, does replying break no contact?**
Yes. Any reply is contact. Not replying, or replying only to practical matters you can't avoid, keeps it intact.

**Do I have to start over if I break no contact?**
Your current streak starts again, but the days you made it still happened and still count toward getting over them. What matters most is going back to no contact quickly.

**Is co-parenting breaking no contact?**
No. With children, full no contact isn't possible, so keep messages short, polite and about the kids only. This is often called limited contact.
