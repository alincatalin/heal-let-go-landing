Your thumb is over "Block". Part of you thinks it's the healthy thing. Part of you thinks it's dramatic, or that you'll regret it, or that they'll think you still care.

Here's how to decide.

## Block, Mute or Unfollow: What's the Difference?

| Option | What it does for you | What they might notice | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mute | Their posts and stories stop showing up | Usually nothing | Most breakups, especially with mutual friends |
| Unfollow | You stop following them | They may notice you're gone | When muting isn't enough |
| Archive their chat | Their messages are out of sight | Nothing | Keeping the chat without seeing it daily |
| Block | They can't contact you or see your profile | Often, if they look for you | Harassment, harm, or when you can't stop checking or texting |

The exact effects differ from app to app, so check the app's own help page if the details matter.

## When Should You Block Your Ex?

Blocking is the right call when:

- **They keep contacting you** after you've asked them to stop.
- **They're harmful.** Threats, cruelty, control, harassment or anything that makes you feel unsafe. Block, keep screenshots of anything serious, and tell someone you trust.
- **You can't stop checking.** If you look at their profile ten times a day, mute isn't enough. In a study of 464 people, those who kept looking at an ex's Facebook page reported more distress, more longing for the ex and less personal growth ([Marshall, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2012.0125)).
- **You keep texting them.** If you've [broken no contact](/blog/articles/i-broke-no-contact/) more than once, a block adds a step between you and the next message.
- **They keep [breadcrumbing](/blog/articles/what-is-breadcrumbing/) you.** Likes, story views and "hey stranger" texts keep the hope alive.

## When Is Muting Enough?

Muting is often the better choice when:

- You share friends, work or children and need to stay reachable for practical things.
- The breakup was fairly calm and blocking would start a new fight.
- You're mostly trying to stop seeing their posts, and you're not tempted to search for them.

Our [no contact rule guide](/blog/articles/no-contact-rule-guide/) recommends muting first for this reason, and blocking when muting isn't working.

## Is Blocking Your Ex Immature?

No. Blocking isn't a message to them. It's a boundary for you.

Contact keeps a breakup going. In a study that followed young adults through the month after a breakup, people who stayed in contact with their ex saw their sadness and love for the ex fade more slowly ([Sbarra and Emery, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1350-4126.2005.00112.x)). If blocking is what keeps you from contact, it's doing its job.

If you want, you can tell them first: "I need some space, so I'm going to block you for a while. Please don't contact me." That's optional. You don't owe an announcement.

## What If You Want to Unblock Them?

The urge to unblock usually comes at night, after a bad day, or when you hear something about them. It's the same urge as wanting to text them, just one step earlier.

Wait at least a day. Ask yourself what you're hoping to find. If the answer is "to see if they miss me" or "to see if they're with someone", unblocking will almost always make you feel worse. Our [should I text my ex quiz](/tools/should-i-text-my-ex/) works for this too.

## FAQ: Blocking Your Ex

**Should I block my ex during no contact?**
If you're struggling to keep no contact or they keep reaching out, yes. If muting and archiving their chat is enough to keep them out of your day, you don't have to.

**Will my ex know I blocked them?**
They may work it out if they try to message you or look at your profile. That's okay. The block is for you, not a message to them.

**Should I block my ex if we have kids together?**
Usually not on the channel you use for the kids. Mute them elsewhere and keep one channel, like a co-parenting app or email, for practical things only.

**Is it okay to unblock my ex later?**
Yes, once seeing their name or posts doesn't pull you back in. If you're not sure yet, it's too soon.
