You check your phone more than you'd admit. You reread their last message for clues. You've searched "will my ex come back" more than once, hoping that one of the results will say yes.

Here's the honest answer, with what research says about when and why exes return, and what to do with your life in the meantime.

## Will My Ex Come Back?

**Maybe. Nobody can promise you either way, and the waiting is what hurts most.** Exes do come back more often than people think. In a US study of nearly 800 young adults, close to half had broken up and gotten back together with a partner at least once ([Halpern-Meekin et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1177/0743558412464524)).

But "they came back" and "it worked this time" are different things. People mostly return because of lingering feelings and attachment ([Dailey et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1080/00224545.2010.503249)), not because whatever ended it has been solved. And on-again, off-again couples tend to report less satisfaction and more uncertainty than couples who never split ([Dailey, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108567756.003)).

So the more useful question is: **would you want them back if nothing had changed?** If the answer is no, then "will they come back" matters much less than it feels like it does right now.

## When Do Exes Usually Come Back?

There's no reliable timeline, but there are common moments:

| When | What's usually going on | What it usually means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first days or weeks | Guilt, shock, missing the routine | Second thoughts, not a plan |
| Late at night, weekends | Loneliness, boredom, a drink | A feeling right now, gone by morning |
| Holidays, birthdays, anniversaries | Nostalgia | Reminders, not change |
| When you've started moving on | They notice you're not waiting | Can be [hoovering](/blog/articles/what-is-hoovering/): wanting you back, for now |
| After their rebound ends | They're hurting and want comfort | You're the familiar option |
| Months later, calm and specific | Real reflection, real change | The one that might be worth listening to |

Notice that only the last row is about a real second chance. Most returns are about how they feel in that moment.

## Will My Ex Come Back After No Contact?

Sometimes. Some people do reach out once the contact stops, because silence makes them notice the gap. But no contact isn't a strategy to get them back, and using it that way usually backfires: you spend thirty days watching your phone instead of healing.

No contact is for you. It's what lets the craving fade and your head clear, so that if they do come back, you can decide with a calm mind. ## Why Waiting for Them Keeps You Stuck

Hope feels kinder than grief. But as long as part of you is waiting, healing is paused.

- **Contact and hope keep love alive.** In a study that followed young adults after a breakup, sadness and love for the ex faded more slowly for people who stayed in contact ([Sbarra and Emery, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1350-4126.2005.00112.x)).
- **Hoping to reunite was linked to harder adjustment.** In a study of 231 young adults after a breakup, not having closure, hoping to get back together and not wanting it to end were most strongly linked to poor adjustment and more rumination ([Saffrey, 2005](https://dspace.library.uvic.ca/items/3f5bfd8f-84d8-47c8-9206-1b7c8fcdce32/full)).
- **Checking keeps it fresh.** People who kept looking at an ex's profile reported more distress and longing ([Marshall, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2012.0125)). Every check for "signs" is a dose of the craving.

This doesn't mean you have to give up hope by force. It means putting the hope on a shelf and living your life in front of it.

## How to Stop Waiting for Your Ex

1. **Decide that your life goes on either way.** Make plans for the next month that don't depend on them.
2. **Stop looking for signs.** Mute their profiles. Ask mutual friends not to pass on news.
3. **Write the question down instead of asking it.** "Is there any chance?" "Do you miss me?" Put it in an [unsent letter](/blog/articles/unsent-letter-to-ex/) or Heal's [unsent chat](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6754834593), where it can't send. The urge usually passes once the words are out.
4. **Set a check-in date with yourself.** In 60 or 90 days, ask: do I still want this person, or did I want the waiting to end? Our [how long should no contact last](/tools/how-long-no-contact/) calculator can help you pick the date.
5. **Remind yourself why it ended.** Write five real moments that didn't work. Read them when the "what if" starts.
6. **Fill the gap with people.** Text a friend instead of opening their profile.

## If They Do Come Back

If your ex reaches out, take your time. You don't owe an instant reply.

- **Ask what's different.** Not "I miss you". What changed, specifically?
- **Watch actions over weeks.** Real change is consistent and respects your pace.
- **Notice how they handle "not yet".** Someone sincere waits. Someone [breadcrumbing](/blog/articles/what-is-breadcrumbing/) or hoovering pushes harder or disappears.

## FAQ: Will My Ex Come Back?

**How do you know if your ex will come back?**
You can't know for sure. Signs that they're thinking of you, like checking your stories or reaching out, don't mean they want the relationship back. See [signs your ex misses you](/blog/articles/signs-your-ex-misses-you/) for what those signals really mean.

**Will he ever come back?**
Some do and some don't, and you can't control which. What you can control is whether your life is on hold while you find out. Put your energy into the things that help you heal, and decide what you want if he returns.

**Will she come back if I give her space?**
Space doesn't guarantee anything. It gives both of you room to see clearly. If she comes back, it should be because she's sure, not because she felt pressured or guilty.

**Do exes come back after months?**
Sometimes. Returns that come months later, calmly and with specific changes, are more worth taking seriously than ones that come in the first weeks or late at night.

**Should I wait for my ex to come back?**
No. Live as if they won't. If they come back, you can decide then. Waiting doesn't make a return more likely, but it does make the pain last longer.

**Why do exes come back when you move on?**
Often because they notice they've lost your attention. Sometimes it's sincere, but often it's about their feelings in that moment. Read [what is hoovering](/blog/articles/what-is-hoovering/) to tell the difference.
